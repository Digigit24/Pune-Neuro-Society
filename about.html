Who We Are

Propagating Advances in Neurology Since 2010

The Neurological Society of Pune (NSP) is a dedicated body of Neurophysicians and Neurosurgeons practicing in the city of Pune.

Established with the vision of fostering academic growth and clinical excellence, NSP serves as a platform for professionals to share knowledge, discuss complex cases, and stay updated with global advancements in neurology.

Since our inception, we have conducted monthly neuro-meets where hospitals across the city present challenging cases. These clinical meetings continue to be the backbone of our society, encouraging interactive discussions and collaborative learning among senior consultants and residents alike.
