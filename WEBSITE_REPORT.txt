14. SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  The Neurological Society of Pune website is a modern, responsive static
  website built with Bootstrap 5, jQuery, and SCSS. It serves as the
  digital presence for a professional medical organization with 70+
  members, established in 2010 in Pune, India.

  The site effectively presents:
    - The organization's mission and history
    - Leadership team and committee structure
    - Distinguished invited faculty members
    - Key activities including monthly Neuromeets and biennial conferences
    - Contact information and engagement channels

  The website uses contemporary web design patterns including video
  backgrounds, scroll animations, responsive layouts, and card-based
  content presentation. It is a static site with no backend, making it
  easy to host and maintain.

  Key areas for improvement include: adding a functional contact form
  backend, implementing analytics tracking, optimizing large assets
  (logo and video), adding Open Graph meta tags for social sharing,
  and implementing structured data for better search engine visibility.
