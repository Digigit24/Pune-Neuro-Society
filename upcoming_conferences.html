Welcome to ICTRIMS 2026

Dear Friends and Colleagues, greetings from the Neurological Society of Pune! We are truly grateful for the tremendous response to ICTRIMS 2026. The enthusiastic interest from colleagues across the country has made this 15th Meeting of ICTRIMS especially exciting for us.

We are delighted to share a detailed Scientific Program featuring an outstanding lineup of internationally and nationally renowned experts. These distinguished faculty members will present the latest advances in the diagnosis and management of central demyelinating and autoimmune neurological diseases.

ICTRIMS 2026 promises cutting-edge lectures, interactive case-based discussions, and meaningful networking opportunities — with practical, clinically relevant insights you can immediately apply in patient care.
