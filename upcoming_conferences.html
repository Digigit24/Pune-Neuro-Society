Welcome to ICTRIMS 2026

Dear Friends and Colleagues, greetings from the Neurological Society of Pune! We are truly grateful for the tremendous response to ICTRIMS 2026. The enthusiastic interest from colleagues across the country has made this 15th Meeting of ICTRIMS especially exciting for us.

We are delighted to share the detailed Scientific Program, featuring an outstanding lineup of internationally and nationally renowned experts. These distinguished faculty members will present the latest advances in the diagnosis and management of central demyelinating and autoimmune neurological diseases.

ICTRIMS 2026 promises to be a highly engaging academic experience, with cutting-edge lectures, interactive case-based discussions, and meaningful networking opportunities with leaders and peers in the field. Most importantly, the sessions are designed to provide practical, clinically relevant insights that you can immediately apply in the day-to-day care of your patients.

If you have not yet completed your registration, we strongly encourage you to do so at the earliest to ensure your participation in this enriching scientific event. We look forward to welcoming you to Pune for an inspiring and memorable ICTRIMS 2026.

Abstract Submission Guidelines

The Scientific Committee invites Abstracts from PG Students and Delegates for Platform Presentations and E-Poster Presentations.

  • Only Registered Delegates can submit abstracts.
  • Deadline: April 30, 2026. Submit as MS Word attachment to neurosocietypune@gmail.com
  • Limit: 250 words, max 5 co-authors.
  • Only the presenting author receives a certificate.
  • Original, unpublished material is encouraged. Case reports accepted only if they bring novel information.
  • Acceptance confirmation by email before May 15, 2026.
